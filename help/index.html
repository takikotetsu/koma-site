Install

Run the installer. The plug-ins and the application go in together.

Quit your host first. The installer stops if Resolve, Premiere, After Effects or Flame is running, and a host that was open keeps the old copy loaded.

Where things land:

OFX (Resolve, Flame)/Library/OFX/Plugins
Adobe (Premiere, After Effects)/Library/Application Support/Adobe/Common/Plug-ins/7.0/MediaCore
Application/Applications

Resolve Studio is required. The free version does not load OFX plug-ins.

Use

  • Resolve — Effects → OpenFX → Filter → KOMA
  • Premiere / After Effects — Effects → KOTETSU → KOMA
  • Flame — OFX → KOMA

Grade before KOMA, not after. KOMA works in display-referred Rec.709; for log footage, pick the format under Input Colorspace and the conversion goes in front.

Colour space

KOMA works in display-referred Rec.709.

InOut
Rec.709 / sRGBunchanged — no conversion either way
Log / Linearconverted to Rec.709
Bypassnothing at all

Only log is one-way. The film stage *is* the scene-to-display rendering, so there is no route back to S-Log3.

HDR (PQ / HLG) and ACES are not supported, and P3 is not offered. Feed KOMA that material and you get a picture and no error — but the colour is wrong.

Update

Run the new installer the same way. Settings, licence and projects all stay.

When the panel reads KOMA 1.2.1 — 1.3 is out, there is a newer version. It checks the site's version number once a day and shows nothing if there is no network.

Licence

The key goes in from the panel. In OFX hosts press Settings…; in Premiere and After Effects press Licence.... Either opens a window of its own, and so does the application's preferences. One key covers the plug-ins and the application.

  • Two machines at a time. To move to a third, release one first — from your

Polar account page or from the application's preferences

  • Activation is online once. After that it renders with no network. It

checks in about once a day when there is one, and a machine that cannot reach us is never stopped by that

  • Before a licence goes in, every feature works and the output carries a

watermark. There is no time limit

When something is wrong

The plug-in does not appear Restart the host. If it still does not, check you are on Resolve Studio — the free version does not load OFX plug-ins.

The installer stops with an error A host is running. Premiere and After Effects can stay in the background after their windows close.

After Effects says the effect is installed more than once An old copy is still there. Delete KOMA.plugin from ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/…/MediaCore if it exists; the one under /Library is the right one.

The watermark will not go away Enter the licence from the panel — Settings…, or Licence... in Adobe — then restart the host. The plug-in reads the licence when it loads.

4K is slow Turning off MTF / Acutance gives back about 9.5 ms at UHD — switch it off while you cut and back on to export. On a laptop, plug in: a low battery throttles the CPU side enough to miss the frame.

After Effects is slower than Premiere After Effects renders on the CPU at present. Premiere and Resolve use the GPU.

Remove

In the application: KOMA → Uninstall KOMA…. The licence and settings stay.

Crossover-1 – 10The three dye layers' curves are not parallel, so the neutral axis bends and the two ends of the scale go opposite ways. It is what a negative printed onto a mismatched stock does, what cross-processing does, and what almost every aged roll does. One control, because one cause moves both ends — two independent tints would be a grade, not this.
Crossover Axis0 – 3600Which way. 0 puts the shadows blue and the highlights yellow, which is the direction ageing takes: the blue-sensitive layer is the top one.
